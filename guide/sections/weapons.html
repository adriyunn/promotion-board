Numbers come with a caveat
Range, rate, and weight figures change with the weapon variant, ammunition, and mount. The figures below are the standard published ones. Check them against the TC on your issued system. If a board member's number differs, say: "First Sergeant, per TC 3-22.9 for my weapon, it's... I'll confirm against the TC."

Must know
Four weapon safety rules: 1) Treat every weapon as loaded. 2) Never point a weapon at anything you don't intend to shoot. 3) Keep your finger straight and off the trigger until you're ready to fire. 4) Keep the weapon on safe until you intend to fire. Verify TC 3-22.9.
Know
Cycle of function (8 steps): Feeding, Chambering, Locking, Firing, Unlocking, Extracting, Ejecting, Cocking.

Know Your optic: ACOG / M150 RCO

M150 Rifle Combat Optic (Trijicon ACOG). 4× magnification. Has an illuminated chevron reticle and a bullet drop compensator with stadia lines for ranges out to 800 m. Know how you zero it and the dimensions you zero at. Verify TC 3-22.9 optic chapter and the unit's zero standard.

Know Malfunctions

Know immediate action and remedial action as written in your current TC. Don't recite older acronyms like "SPORTS" unless you know your TC still uses them. Be ready to say what a stoppage, malfunction, and misfire are. Verify TC 3-22.9.
