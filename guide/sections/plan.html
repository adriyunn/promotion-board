Before you leave the house

  • AGSU on, inspected, lint-rolled
  • Shoes shined. Spare shirt in the car.
  • Fresh haircut. Shaved that morning.
  • CAC / ID
  • Lint roller, small sewing kit
  • Water. Something light to eat.

Outside the board room

  • Arrive 30+ minutes early
  • Final mirror check: brass, ribbons, gig line
  • Say the creed once in your head
  • Deep breaths. Slow down.
  • Phone off and out of your pockets
  • Knock once, firmly
Open items before 20 November
  • Get the MOI: uniform details, reporting procedure, creed, topics
  • Confirm every name in Section {{REF:coc}}
  • IVY 7, the CSM's and commander's philosophy, unit honors, company nickname
  • Your M4 zero, your ACOG model, your issued IFAK contents
  • Your unit SARC/VA, EOL, and chaplain names
  • Compare the creed text word for word against army.mil
