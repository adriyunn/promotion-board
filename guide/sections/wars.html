Major Wars & Unit Combat History

The Army's major wars, and where 4ID and the 12th Infantry fought in each.

America's major wars

WarYears (U.S.)Key facts for the board
Revolutionary War1775–1783The Army (Continental Army) was born on 14 June 1775. Von Steuben's Blue Book defined NCO duties.
War of 18121812–1815Against Britain. The defense of Fort McHenry inspired "The Star-Spangled Banner."
Mexican-American War1846–1848Many Civil War leaders first saw combat here.
Civil War1861–1865The bloodiest U.S. war. The Medal of Honor was created (Army version 1862). The 12th Infantry was constituted in 1861.
Spanish-American War1898Cuba and the Philippines. 12th Infantry at El Caney.
Philippine-American War1899–190212th Infantry served in the Philippines. The sea lion on the regimental crest comes from this.
World War I1917–19184ID organized in Dec 1917. Fought in the Aisne-Marne, St. Mihiel and Meuse-Argonne campaigns.
World War II1941–19454ID landed first at Utah Beach, 6 June 1944 (D-Day). Fought at Cherbourg, the Hürtgen Forest and the Battle of the Bulge.
Korean War1950–1953Ended in an armistice, not a peace treaty. U.S. forces are still in South Korea.
Vietnam WarAbout 1965–1973 (major U.S. combat)4ID deployed 1966–1970 to the Central Highlands. 2-12 IN served in Vietnam (with the 25th ID). Verify

4th Infantry Division in combat Verify

Know
WWI: Aisne-Marne, St. Mihiel, Meuse-Argonne.
Must know
WWII: The 8th, 12th and 22nd Infantry Regiments made up 4ID. They landed first on Utah Beach. BG Theodore Roosevelt Jr. landed with the first wave and received the Medal of Honor. Then Cherbourg, the liberation of Paris, the Hürtgen Forest and the Battle of the Bulge in Luxembourg.
Know
Vietnam: 1966–1970. Central Highlands.
Must know
OIF: 4ID Soldiers captured Saddam Hussein near Tikrit on 13 December 2003 (Operation Red Dawn).
Know
OEF: multiple deployments to Afghanistan. The Dockery Medal of Honor action (C Co, 2-12 IN) was in Kapisa Province.

12th Infantry Regiment Verify

Know
Civil War: fought with the Regular Army ("the Regulars") in the eastern campaigns, including Gettysburg.
Know
Spanish-American War: El Caney, Cuba, 1898. The crest commemorates capturing a Spanish flag there.
Must know
WWII: one of 4ID's three infantry regiments. Landed at Utah Beach, fought in the Hürtgen Forest, and held the line in Luxembourg during the Battle of the Bulge.
Know
GWOT: 2-12 IN deployed to Iraq and Afghanistan. Became a Stryker battalion in 2SBCT, 4ID in 2021.
Know
Recent: Joint Task Force-Southern Border (2025). NTC rotation (2026).

Board answers
Where did 4ID land on D-Day?
Utah Beach, 6 June 1944. It was the first division ashore there.
Who received the Medal of Honor for Utah Beach?
BG Theodore Roosevelt Jr.
Which 4ID unit captured Saddam Hussein, and when?
4ID Soldiers, near Tikrit, 13 December 2003, during Operation Red Dawn.
What were 4ID's three infantry regiments in WWII?
The 8th, 12th and 22nd Infantry Regiments.
What does OEF stand for? OIF?
Operation Enduring Freedom (Afghanistan). Operation Iraqi Freedom (Iraq).
When did the Revolutionary War start?
1775. The Army's birthday is 14 June 1775.
