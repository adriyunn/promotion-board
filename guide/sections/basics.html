Army Basics

Organization, ranks, the flag, and the Army's mission. A 1SG asks these when they want to see if you know the fundamentals.
Army mission Must know
"To deploy, fight, and win our Nation's wars by providing ready, prompt, and sustained land dominance by Army forces across the full spectrum of conflict as part of the joint force." Verify army.mil/about

Army components

Know
Three components: Regular Army (active), Army National Guard, and U.S. Army Reserve.
Know
Three types of BCT: Infantry (IBCT), Stryker (SBCT), and Armored (ABCT).

Flag & National Anthem Verify

Know
13 stripes stand for the original 13 colonies. 50 stars stand for the states.
Know
A ceremonial fold makes 13 folds into a triangle with only the blue field showing.
Know
Half-staff: raise it to the peak first, then lower it to half-staff. Raise it to the peak again before lowering it at the end of the day.
Know
National anthem, in uniform outdoors: face the flag (or the music), stand at attention, and salute on the first note until the last note.
Know
Indoors in uniform: stand at attention facing the flag or music. No salute.
Bonus
The Army flag was dedicated on 14 June 1956. The Army song, "The Army Goes Rolling Along," was adopted the same year.
Board answers
What is the Army's mission?
To deploy, fight, and win our Nation's wars by providing ready, prompt, and sustained land dominance by Army forces across the full spectrum of conflict as part of the joint force.
How many Soldiers are in a fire team? A squad?
A fire team has 4. A squad has 9: the squad leader and two fire teams.
What's the difference between a CPL and a SPC?
Both are E-4. The CPL is an NCO with NCO authority and responsibilities. The SPC is not.
What are the three Army components?
Regular Army, Army National Guard, Army Reserve.
What does the flag stand for?
13 stripes for the original colonies. 50 stars for the states.
What do you do when the anthem plays outdoors and you're in uniform?
Face the flag or the music, stand at attention, and salute from the first note to the last.
