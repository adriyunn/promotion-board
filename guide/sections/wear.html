Wear & Appearance

You're wearing the subject of these questions. Be ready to be tested on your own uniform.
Must know
AR 670-1, Wear and Appearance of Army Uniforms and Insignia. DA Pam 670-1 is the detailed wear guide. AR 670-1 Chapter 3 covers appearance and grooming.
Must know
Facial hair: Army Directives 2025-13 and 2026-09. Male Soldiers are clean-shaven in uniform or on duty unless an approved medical exception or religious accommodation applies. Authorized mustaches are still permitted. An existing approved religious accommodation stays valid during its required review.
Know
Army Directive 2025-18 also updated grooming and appearance. For any specific hairstyle, jewelry, tattoo, or uniform question, check the AR, DA Pam, directive, and 4ID Green Book together.
Know
4ID Green Book (9 Apr 2026): local tactical uniform standards, Ivy Duty / Ivy Light / Ivy Ready. Ivy Ready requires two tourniquets and an accessible issued IFAK in training and operations.
Know
AGSU: ribbons centered 1/8 inch above the left breast pocket of the coat, right to left by precedence, no more than 4 per row. No ribbons on the AGSU shirt. No miniature badges. Verify DA Pam 670-1.
Board answers
What is the regulation for wear and appearance?
AR 670-1, with wear details in DA Pam 670-1.
What chapter of AR 670-1 covers grooming?
Chapter 3.
What is the facial hair standard?
Clean-shaven in uniform or on duty unless an approved medical exception or religious accommodation applies.
Where do ribbons go on your AGSU coat?
Centered 1/8 inch above the left breast pocket, highest precedence on the wearer's right.
What are the three 4ID tactical uniform configurations?
Ivy Duty, Ivy Light, Ivy Ready (4ID Green Book).
