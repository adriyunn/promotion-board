ProwordMeaning

WAIT / WAIT OUTPause a few seconds / I'll call you back.
CORRECTIONI made an error. The correct version follows.
RADIO CHECKWhat is my signal strength and readability?
Never say "Over and out"
OVER asks for a reply. OUT ends with no reply. Together they contradict each other.

Radios Verify

Know
SINCGARS = Single Channel Ground and Airborne Radio System. VHF-FM, frequency hopping. Being replaced by newer handheld and manpack radios such as the AN/PRC-163 and AN/PRC-117G.
Know
SOI (Signal Operating Instructions) give the frequencies, call signs, challenge and password.
Know
PACE plan: Primary, Alternate, Contingency, Emergency methods of communication.
Know
Radio check reply: "Lima Charlie" means Loud and Clear.
Bonus
COMSEC = Communications Security. Never send classified or sensitive info in the clear.
Local Know the radios in your vehicle and your platoon's call signs.

Maintenance

Must know
PMCS = Preventive Maintenance Checks and Services. Intervals: Before, During, After operation, plus Weekly and Monthly. Done from the operator's -10 TM.
Know
DA Form 5988-E = Equipment Maintenance and Inspection Worksheet, generated from GCSS-Army. DA Form 2404 = the manual version.
Know
Two maintenance levels: Field (operator/crew and unit maintainers) and Sustainment (depot and repair-and-return). Verify DA Pam 750-1.
Know
GCSS-Army = Global Combat Support System–Army: the system for maintenance and supply.
Know
Deadline (NMC): a fault that makes the vehicle Not Mission Capable. It can't be dispatched until fixed.
Know
Dispatch: PMCS done, faults recorded, a licensed operator, and a dispatch issued before the vehicle moves.
Know
Ground guides: required when backing up, in motor pools, in assembly areas, and in congested areas. Two ground guides for tracked or large vehicles when backing. Verify unit SOP.
Know
Property: DA Form 2062 = hand receipt. DD Form 200 = Financial Liability Investigation of Property Loss (FLIPL). Policy: AR 735-5.
Board answers
What does PMCS stand for, and what are the intervals?
Preventive Maintenance Checks and Services. Before, during, after, weekly, and monthly.
What form is used to record faults?
DA Form 5988-E. DA Form 2404 is the manual version.
What's the difference between ROGER and WILCO?
ROGER means I received it. WILCO means I received it, understand it, and will comply.
What does PACE stand for?
Primary, Alternate, Contingency, Emergency.
What is a hand receipt?
DA Form 2062. It shows who is responsible for property.
What does SINCGARS stand for?
Single Channel Ground and Airborne Radio System.
