The Stryker (ICVV) New

Know
ATP 3-21.9, Stryker Infantry Rifle Platoon and Squad (24 Jun 2025), is the Stryker companion to ATP 3-21.8.
Know
ICVV = Infantry Carrier Vehicle, Double V-Hull (M1256). It's the DVH version of the M1126 ICV. The V-shaped hull deflects IED blast. Crew of 2 (driver and vehicle commander), carrying a 9-Soldier infantry squad. Armed with a Remote Weapon Station (M2 .50 cal or Mk 19). Verify TM and unit config.
Know
PMCS: Before, During, After, Weekly, Monthly, per the vehicle's -10 operator TM. Faults go on DA Form 5988-E. Maintenance policy is in DA Pam 750-1 (29 Apr 2026). Verify
Use your experience
You were a driver. If you're asked about the Stryker, talk about PMCS, dispatch, the 5988-E, and ground guides. "I've been a driver" is a strong answer when you can show you know the standard.

Follow-ups the 1SGs ask New
What are the four steps of a battle drill react to contact?
React, locate, suppress, and then the leader decides to assault, flank or break contact. Verify ATP 3-21.8.
What's the difference between cover and concealment?
Cover protects you from fire. Concealment only hides you from observation.
What are the three types of patrols?
Combat, reconnaissance, and security patrols. Verify
What are the forms of maneuver?
Envelopment, flank attack, frontal attack, infiltration, penetration, turning movement. Verify ADP 3-90.
What is a team leader's role in a Stryker squad dismount?
Control the fire team, keep it in formation and contact, and execute the squad leader's plan.
