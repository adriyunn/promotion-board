Board answers
What are the two attack modes of the Javelin?
Top attack and direct attack.
What does TOW stand for?
Tube-launched, Optically tracked, Wire-guided.
What is the kill radius of an M67 grenade?
5 meters. The casualty radius is 15 meters.
What is written on the front of a Claymore?
"FRONT TOWARD ENEMY."
What mortar does the Stryker MCV carry?
The M121 120 mm mortar.
What's the max effective range of the AT4?
About 300 meters.
