Leadership & Counseling

As a team leader, counseling is what you do every day. Expect scenario questions.
Must know
Army leadership is the activity of influencing people by providing purpose, direction, and motivation to accomplish the mission and improve the organization. (ADP 6-22)

Must know Attributes (what a leader IS)

CCharacterPPresenceIIntellect
Character: Army Values, empathy, Warrior Ethos/service ethos, discipline, humility. Presence: military and professional bearing, fitness, confidence, resilience. Intellect: mental agility, sound judgment, innovation, interpersonal tact, expertise. Verify ADP 6-22 Ch 2–4

Must know Competencies (what a leader DOES)

LLeadsDDevelopsAAchieves
Leads: others, builds trust, extends influence, leads by example, communicates. Develops: creates a positive environment, prepares self, develops others, stewards the profession. Achieves: gets results. Verify ADP 6-22 Ch 5–7

Counseling: ATP 6-22.1 (13 Feb 2024)

Must know
Three categories: Performance, Professional Growth, Event. (Ch 2, para 2-4)
Must know
DA Form 4856, Developmental Counseling Form, documents counseling.
Know
Event counseling examples: specific instances (good or bad), reception and integration, crisis, referral, promotion, separation. Verify
Know
Three approaches: Nondirective, Directive, Combined. Verify
Know
Four-stage counseling process: 1) Identify the need for counseling 2) Prepare 3) Conduct the session 4) Follow up. Verify
Know
Conducting the session: open the session, discuss the issues, develop a plan of action, record and close. Verify
Bonus
Counselor qualities: respect, self-awareness and cultural awareness, credibility, empathy. Verify
Know
Local: the 4ID Yellow Book (19 Feb 2026) is the local counseling SOP. It supplements Army doctrine and does not replace it.

Evaluations: AR 623-3 (14 Feb 2025)

Know
DA Form 2166-9-1 = NCOER for SGT. DA Form 2166-9-1A = NCOER Support Form. Neither one is a counseling form. DA Pam 623-3 is the procedural pamphlet.
Board answers
Define leadership.
Influencing people by providing purpose, direction, and motivation to accomplish the mission and improve the organization.
Name the three leader attributes and the three competencies.
Attributes: Character, Presence, Intellect. Competencies: Leads, Develops, Achieves.
What are the three categories of counseling?
Performance, professional growth, and event.
What form is used for counseling? For a SGT's NCOER?
DA Form 4856. DA Form 2166-9-1.
When should you do an initial counseling?
When a Soldier first arrives (reception and integration), and within the first 30 days of a rating period. Verify AR 623-3
Follow-ups the 1SGs ask New
What are the levels of leadership?
Direct, organizational, and strategic. A team leader works at the direct level. Verify ADP 6-22.
What's the difference between counseling, coaching and mentoring?
Counseling: a leader and subordinate review performance and potential. Coaching: guiding someone to improve a specific skill. Mentoring: a voluntary, long-term relationship between someone more experienced and someone less experienced, for development.
What should a counseling plan of action include?
Specific actions, who is responsible, and a deadline, so the Soldier knows exactly what to do to improve.
What is the NCOER rating chain for a SGT?
Rater, senior rater, and supplementary reviewer, if one applies. Verify AR 623-3.
What does it mean to lead by example?
Meeting every standard you enforce: PT, appearance, timeliness. Soldiers do what they see.
