Board answers
What is the NCO support channel?
The channel from the SMA down to the first-line leader. It parallels and reinforces the chain of command. NCOs use it to pass information, standards, and individual training and welfare issues. Verify AR 600-20, Ch 2.
Follow-ups the 1SGs ask New
Why is it important to know your chain of command?
So you know who gives orders and who is responsible, and so issues get routed to the right level.
What's the difference between the chain of command and the NCO support channel?
The chain of command issues orders and holds command authority. The NCO support channel backs up the chain, focusing on standards, training and Soldier care.
Who is the senior enlisted leader of 4ID?
The Division CSM. Verify name.
