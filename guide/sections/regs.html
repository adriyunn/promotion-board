Know UCMJ basics Verify

Art. 15: Nonjudicial punishment. Types: summarized, company grade, field grade.
Art. 31: Rights against self-incrimination.
Courts-martial: summary, special, general.
Art. 86: AWOL. Art. 91: insubordinate conduct toward a warrant officer or NCO. Art. 92: failure to obey an order or regulation. Art. 134: the General Article.
Board answers
What regulation covers Army Command Policy?
AR 600-20.
What is AER?
Army Emergency Relief. It provides financial help to Soldiers and their families. AR 930-4.
What do BOSS and MWR stand for?
Better Opportunities for Single Soldiers; Morale, Welfare, and Recreation.
What does a flag do?
It suspends favorable personnel actions such as promotion, awards, and schools. AR 600-8-2.
What article of the UCMJ covers nonjudicial punishment?
Article 15.
Follow-ups the 1SGs ask New
What regulation covers leave?
AR 600-8-10. Soldiers earn 2.5 days per month.
What does a flag do to promotions?
It suspends favorable actions, including promotion. AR 600-8-2.
What are the types of discharge?
Administrative: honorable, general, other than honorable. Punitive (court-martial only): bad conduct and dishonorable.
Can a Soldier be punished for self-referring to ASAP?
Self-referral is encouraged and handled differently from command or test-based referrals. Verify AR 600-85 limited use policy.
What article covers sexual assault?
Article 120, UCMJ.
