Training, Risk Management & CBRN

Sergeants plan and run training. The board wants to see you can do it safely and to standard.

8-Step Training Model Must know

  1. Plan the training
  2. Train and certify leaders
  3. Recon the training site
  4. Issue the order
  5. Rehearse
  6. Execute
  7. Conduct an After Action Review (AAR)
  8. Retrain
Verify FM 7-0, Training (14 Jun 2021). ADP 7-0 covers training principles. AR 350-1 is the training regulation.

After Action Review Know

  1. What was supposed to happen?
  2. What happened?
  3. Why did it happen?
  4. What do we sustain, and what do we improve?
Know
AARs can be formal or informal. They focus on performance against the standard, not on blaming people.
Know
Crawl–Walk–Run: learn the task, practice it, then perform it under realistic conditions.
Bonus
METL = Mission Essential Task List: the tasks the unit must be able to do for its mission. Task proficiency ratings: T (trained), P (practiced), U (untrained). Verify FM 7-0.

Risk Management: ATP 5-19 Must know

  1. Identify the hazards
  2. Assess the hazards
  3. Develop controls and make risk decisions
  4. Implement controls
  5. Supervise and evaluate
Know
Document it on DD Form 2977, the Deliberate Risk Assessment Worksheet. Risk levels: Extremely High, High, Medium, Low. Verify
Know
Safety program: AR 385-10. Range safety: AR 385-63.

OPSEC: AR 530-1

Know
OPSEC = Operations Security: protecting critical information from adversaries. That includes social media, unit movements, photos, and duty schedules.
Bonus
OPSEC process: identify critical information, analyze threats, analyze vulnerabilities, assess risk, apply countermeasures. Verify

Board answers
Name the 8-step training model.
Plan; train and certify leaders; recon the site; issue the order; rehearse; execute; conduct the AAR; retrain.
What are the four parts of an AAR?
What was supposed to happen, what happened, why it happened, and what to sustain or improve.
What are the five steps of risk management?
Identify hazards; assess hazards; develop controls and make risk decisions; implement controls; supervise and evaluate.
What form documents a deliberate risk assessment?
DD Form 2977.
What do you wear in MOPP 4?
Everything: overgarment, boots, mask and gloves.
What regulation covers OPSEC?
AR 530-1.
