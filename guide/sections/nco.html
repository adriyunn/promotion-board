NCO History, Duties & Personal Questions

These are where the board decides whether you're ready to lead. Prepare your answers. Don't wing them.

"Tell the board about yourself" (60–90 seconds)

Structure

  1. Name, rank, MOS, unit, position. "Specialist ____, 11B, Team Leader in 2nd Platoon, C Company, 2-12 Infantry."
  2. Hometown and time in service.
  3. Your career so far: M320 grenadier, Stryker driver, SAW gunner and rifleman, now team leader.
  4. One or two accomplishments: schools, ranges, rotations (NTC, the border mission), awards.
  5. Goals: BLC, civilian education, next position.
  6. Close: "...and that is a little about me, Sergeant Major."
Leave out family drama, politics, and anything you can't defend.

Questions to prepare answers for

QuestionWhat a strong answer includes
Why should you be promoted?Evidence you already perform at the next level, such as leading your fire team or training Soldiers on the 320 and driving. Show you know the standard, plus one specific example.
What does it mean to be an NCO?Connect it to the creed: the Backbone of the Army, the two basic responsibilities, leading by example, and enforcing standards.
What will you do in your first 30 days as a SGT?Get to know your Soldiers (initial counseling, family, goals). Learn your equipment and task status. Set your standards. Meet your leadership's intent.
One of your Soldiers comes to you with a problem you can't fix. What do you do?Listen. Make sure they're safe. Use the right resource (chaplain, MFLC, Behavioral Health, SHARP, AER). Inform your chain. Follow up.
A Soldier is late for formation for the second time.Find out why. Do event counseling on DA Form 4856. Apply corrective training if appropriate. Inform your squad leader. Follow up.
You see another NCO correcting a Soldier in a way that crosses the line.Step in professionally, privately if possible. Report it through the NCO support channel. Standards apply to everyone.
What's your biggest weakness?A real weakness, plus what you're doing to fix it.

NCO history & duties

Know
The NCO Corps dates to 1775 with the Continental Army. Baron von Steuben's "Blue Book" (Regulations for the Order and Discipline of the Troops of the United States, 1778–79) first defined NCO duties in writing.
Know
The first Sergeant Major of the Army was SMA William O. Wooldridge (1966).
Bonus
The NCO Creed was written in the early 1970s at Fort Benning, as part of the NCO education system. Verify
Know
TC 7-22.7, The Noncommissioned Officer Guide (19 Aug 2025), is the NCO's reference for duties, responsibilities, and authority. TC 7-21.13, The Soldier's Guide (17 Sep 2025), is the Soldier-level reference.
Know
NCOs carry out the commander's intent. They lead Soldiers, train individuals and teams, maintain standards and discipline, and take care of Soldiers and their equipment.
Follow-ups the 1SGs ask New
Who was the first Sergeant Major of the Army?
SMA William O. Wooldridge, 1966.
What is the "Backbone of the Army"?
The NCO Corps.
What is the NCO Guide?
TC 7-22.7, The Noncommissioned Officer Guide.
Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
A realistic goal: squad leader and SSG, ALC complete, college progress, and developing Soldiers.
What makes a good team leader?
Leading by example, knowing your Soldiers, enforcing standards consistently, and training your team to be ready to take your job.
