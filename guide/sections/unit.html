Medal of Honor: Company C, 2-12 Infantry
On 18 June 2026, President Trump presented the Medal of Honor to MAJ (Ret.) Nicholas Dockery at the White House. He was a Platoon Leader in Company C, 2nd Battalion, 12th Infantry Regiment, in Kapisa Province, Afghanistan. The award upgraded his Silver Star. He led a counterattack to clear a courtyard and shielded a Soldier from an enemy grenade blast.
