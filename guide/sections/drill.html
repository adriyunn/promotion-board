Customs & courtesies Verify

Know
Salute commissioned and warrant officers, the Medal of Honor recipient, the flag during the anthem or colors, and during reveille and retreat.
Know
Generally no salute: indoors (except when formally reporting), in the field or under tactical conditions, when both hands are full, or when the act would be impractical. Follow AR 600-25.
Know
Reveille and retreat: face the flag (or the sound of music), stand at attention, and salute on the first note. In a vehicle, stop and sit at attention.
Know
When an NCO addresses you: stand at parade rest for a senior NCO and answer "Sergeant" or "First Sergeant."
Withheld
Your old notes listed holiday gun-salute numbers and indoor-salute exceptions with no source. Don't memorize those. If asked, cite AR 600-25.
Board answers
What manual covers drill and ceremonies?
TC 3-21.5.
What regulation covers salutes and honors?
AR 600-25, Salutes, Honors, and Courtesy.
What are the two parts of most drill commands?
The preparatory command and the command of execution.
How long is a quick-time step, and at what cadence?
30 inches at 120 steps per minute.
Follow-ups the 1SGs ask New
Name the four rest positions.
Parade rest, stand at ease, at ease, rest.
When do you not salute?
Indoors (except when formally reporting), in the field or under tactical conditions, or when both hands are full. Verify AR 600-25.
What is the double-time cadence?
180 steps per minute, 30 inches.
