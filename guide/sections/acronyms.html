Board answers
What is an ORP?
Objective Rally Point. It's where the unit halts out of sight, sound and small-arms range of the objective to make final preparations.
What's the difference between CASEVAC and MEDEVAC?
MEDEVAC uses a dedicated medical platform with medical care en route. CASEVAC uses any available, non-medical vehicle.
What is a FRAGORD?
A fragmentary order, which changes an existing order.
What does LD stand for?
Line of Departure. It's where the attack begins and is crossed at a set time.
What are PCCs and PCIs?
Pre-combat checks and pre-combat inspections. Leaders confirm Soldiers and equipment are ready before the mission.
What is an FPL?
A Final Protective Line: the line where a machine gun's grazing fire stops an enemy assault.
