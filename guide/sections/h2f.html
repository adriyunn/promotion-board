Must know AFT: 5 events

  1. 3-Repetition Maximum Deadlift (MDL)
  2. Hand-Release Push-Up (HRP)
  3. Sprint-Drag-Carry (SDC)
  4. Plank (PLK)
  5. Two-Mile Run (2MR)
Replaced the ACFT on 1 Jun 2025. Combat standard (11B): 350 total, minimum 60 per event, sex-neutral. Enforced for the Regular Army since 1 Jan 2026. Verify ATP 7-22.01.

Must know CFT: 7 continuous events

  1. 1-mile run
  2. 30 dead-stop push-ups
  3. 100-meter sprint
  4. 16 × 40-lb sandbag lifts to 65 inches
  5. Two 40-lb water cans, 50 meters
  6. 25 m high crawl + 25 m rush
  7. 1-mile run
Pass/fail on total time. The Army FAQ says 30 minutes or less. Verify uniform and event details in the directive.
Army Directive 2026-07 (17 Apr 2026): applies to 11B
Annual framework for active-duty combat specialties: one CFT and one AFT per year, at least four months apart.
Year one is an implementation period: no adverse administrative action solely for a CFT failure. Full implementation is 365 days after the directive.
So in November 2026, a CFT failure alone does not trigger adverse action.

Board answers
Name the five H2F domains.
Physical, nutritional, mental, spiritual, and sleep readiness.
Name the five AFT events.
Deadlift, hand-release push-up, sprint-drag-carry, plank, two-mile run.
What's the AFT combat standard?
350 total points with at least 60 on each event.
Does a CFT failure in November 2026 trigger adverse action?
Not on its own. AD 2026-07 gives a one-year implementation period from 17 Apr 2026.
What are the last three exercises of the Recovery Drill?
Groin Stretch, Calf Stretch, Hamstring Stretch.
