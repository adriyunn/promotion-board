Land Navigation

TC 3-25.26, Map Reading and Land Navigation (15 Nov 2013). Not FM 3-25.26.

Must know 5 Major

Hill · Saddle · Valley · Ridge · Depression
Cue: "Hidden Valley Ranch Salad Dressing" (different order, same 5).

Must know 3 Minor

Draw · Spur · Cliff

Must know 2 Supplementary

Cut · Fill

Map colors Verify

ColorShows
BlackCultural (man-made) features other than roads
Red-BrownOn newer maps: combined cultural, relief, and elevation features (contour lines readable under red light)
BlueWater: lakes, rivers, swamps
GreenVegetation with military significance: woods, orchards
BrownRelief features and elevation (contours on older maps)
RedCultural features: populated areas, main roads, boundaries (older maps)
OtherSpecial information, explained in the marginal information
Know
Three norths: True, Magnetic, Grid. The declination diagram shows the angles between them.
Know
Grid precision: 6-digit = 100 m. 8-digit = 10 m. Read right, then up.
Know
Contour lines: Index (heavier, numbered), Intermediate, Supplementary (dashed).
Know
Lensatic compass: 360 degrees or 6400 mils. Holding techniques: center-hold and compass-to-cheek.
Know
Intersection finds an unknown point using two known points. Resection finds your own location using two or more known points.
Bonus
Standard military map scale: 1:50,000. Know your own pace count per 100 m.
Board answers
Name the five major terrain features.
Hill, saddle, valley, ridge, depression.
Minor and supplementary?
Minor: draw, spur, cliff. Supplementary: cut, fill.
An 8-digit grid is accurate to how many meters?
10 meters.
What are the three norths?
True, magnetic, and grid north.
Follow-ups the 1SGs ask New
What is the scale of a standard military map?
1:50,000.
What do you use to determine your location?
Resection, using two or more known points.
How many mils in a circle?
6,400.
