First Aid, TCCC & 9-Line

Your 1SGs will expect a team leader to know how to keep a Soldier alive.
Must know
ATP 4-02.11, Casualty Response, Tactical Combat Casualty Care, and First Aid (23 Mar 2026). It superseded TC 4-02.1.

1 · Care Under Fire

Return fire and win the fight. Move the casualty to cover. Direct them to self-aid. A tourniquet for life-threatening extremity bleeding is the only treatment under fire. Verify

2 · Tactical Field Care

You're no longer under effective fire. Assess and treat with MARCH. Verify

3 · Tactical Evacuation Care

Care while the casualty is moved to a higher level of care. Fixed This is not the "evac plan."

MARCH is the standard TCCC assessment sequence. Verify against ATP 4-02.11 and your unit's medical training.
Medical answers follow current medical training
Don't work out a treatment from a mnemonic. Rehearse IFAK contents and casualty transfer with your platoon medic. Know where your IFAK and both tourniquets are on your kit (4ID Ivy Ready).

Board answers
What replaced TC 4-02.1?
ATP 4-02.11, Casualty Response, Tactical Combat Casualty Care, and First Aid.
Name the three phases of TCCC.
Care Under Fire, Tactical Field Care, Tactical Evacuation Care.
What does MARCH stand for?
Massive hemorrhage, Airway, Respiration, Circulation, Hypothermia/Head injury.
What is line 1 of a 9-line?
Location of the pickup site.
Follow-ups the 1SGs ask New
Where do you place a tourniquet in Care Under Fire?
High and tight on the injured limb. Verify ATP 4-02.11.
What is the most preventable cause of death on the battlefield?
Bleeding from an extremity (massive hemorrhage).
What's in your IFAK?
Know your issued contents: tourniquet, pressure dressing, hemostatic gauze, nasopharyngeal airway, chest seal, etc. Local
What does precedence A, Urgent, mean?
Evacuate within 1 hour.
