Benefits & Resilience

A team leader sends Soldiers to the right resource. Boards check that you know what's available.
Dollar amounts change
Education caps, TSP matching and insurance amounts change by fiscal year and policy. The structure below is stable, but confirm dollar figures with the education center, finance or ArmyIgnitED before you quote them.

Education

Know Tuition Assistance (TA)

Pays tuition for off-duty college courses through ArmyIgnitED. It has a per-credit-hour cap and an annual cap. Governed by AR 621-5, the Army Continuing Education System. Start at the installation Education Center.

Know Credentialing Assistance (CA)

Pays for civilian certifications and licenses related to your career, also through ArmyIgnitED.

Know GI Bill

The Post-9/11 GI Bill pays for education after qualifying service. It can be transferred to dependents if you meet the service requirements.

Bonus SkillBridge

Civilian job training during your last 180 days of service, with commander approval.

Pay, retirement & insurance

Know
BRS (Blended Retirement System): the Government puts in 1% automatically and matches your Thrift Savings Plan (TSP) contributions up to 4% more, for 5% total when you contribute 5%. It also includes a pension after 20 years and continuation pay mid-career. Verify
Know
SGLI (Servicemembers' Group Life Insurance): low-cost life insurance, maximum $500,000. Keep your beneficiaries current through SOES. Verify
Know
AER (Army Emergency Relief): no-interest loans and grants for emergencies such as rent, car repair or emergency travel. AR 930-4.

Board answers
A Soldier wants to start college. Where do you send them?
To the Education Center to set up Tuition Assistance through ArmyIgnitED. It's governed by AR 621-5.
What does the government contribute under BRS?
1% automatically, plus matching up to 5% total when the Soldier contributes 5% to the TSP.
What's the maximum SGLI coverage?
$500,000.
What does ATC stand for in MRT?
Activating event, Thoughts, Consequences.
Name the six MRT competencies.
Self-awareness, self-regulation, optimism, mental agility, strengths of character, connection.
Where can a Soldier get confidential counseling with no records?
The chaplain (privileged communication) or an MFLC.
